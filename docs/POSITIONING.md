# Positioning

## Category

The first **agentic multi-asset UHNW desk**: superyachts, private jets, luxury estates.

Not a marketplace. Not a charter app. Not a CRM with a nicer coat.

## Who it is for

- Principals and family officers acting for UHNW and billionaire families
- Counsel and chiefs of staff who already gate access
- Owners who will only show an asset to a verified counterpart

Who it is not for: retail charterers, listing browsers, lead-gen brokers.

## The sentence

Meridian Desk is a private, invitation-only brokerage that uses software agents to match UHNW and family-office mandates for yachts, jets and estates, while named human advisors run diligence and close.

## Four words, kept literal

| Word | Meaning here |
| --- | --- |
| Private | Off-market default. No public catalogue. Reveals are logged. |
| Direct | Principal or designated officer to a named advisor. No floor of juniors. |
| Fast | Intake to scored shortlist to diligence pack in one desk run. |
| Trusted | Human gate, scoped officers, audit trail, humans close. |

## Competitive frame

Traditional houses are split by asset class. Meridian's wedge is the combined mandate plus the human gate.

## WorldAuth principles, applied

- Proof of human at the gate, not KYC dumped into a marketing list
- Passkeys / hosted auth later; invite codes now
- Officers inherit scoped access, not the principal's full graph
- Matching layer sees constraints, not gossip

## WorldAEO principles, applied

- One entity: Meridian Desk
- One set of canonical answers on `/answers`
- FAQ and Organization schema
- `llms.txt` for retrieval
- Public site is the citation surface; the desk is robots-disallowed
